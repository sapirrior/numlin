Mathematical Operations

Universal Functions (uFuncs)

Unary operations that operate element-wise on both Vectors and Matrices.

  • nl.sqrt(arr): Square root.
  • nl.exp(arr): Exponential.
  • nl.log(arr): Natural logarithm.

Statistics

Available under the nl.statistics namespace.

  • sum(arr): Total sum of elements, optimized with ILP.
  • mean(arr): Arithmetic mean.

Random

Available under the nl.random namespace.

  • vector(length, dtype?): Vector of uniform random values [0, 1).
  • matrix(rows, cols, dtype?): Matrix of uniform random values [0, 1).
